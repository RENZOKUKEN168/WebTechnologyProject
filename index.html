Some fun facts:

There are roughly 400,000 identified species of plants on Earth, whereby the vast majority - around 300,000 species, reproduce by producing seeds.

Green plants produce a large quantity of the world's molecular oxygen and serve as the foundation for Earth's ecosystems.

Plants that yield grain, fruit and vegetables have established as the staples of human diet and have been cultivated by people for thousands of years.

Plants serve as many cultural and practical purposes, use as decorative elements, construction materials and writing surfaces (paper). They are also extremely important to human beings, as they have long been the sources of medicine and drugs.
