Dipterocarpaceae · Genus 01

Vatica

Vatica is a genus of tropical Asian rainforest trees. Its members commonly produce resinous timber and fruits with enlarged, wing-like sepals.

Selected species: Vatica borneensis · Vatica flavida

Dipterocarpaceae · Genus 02

Dipterocarpus

Dipterocarpus contains many tall canopy and emergent trees of Asian rainforests. The name refers to their distinctive two-winged fruits.

Selected species: Dipterocarpus alatus · Dipterocarpus grandiflorus

Lauraceae · Genus 01

Actinodaphne

Actinodaphne is a genus of evergreen trees and shrubs in the laurel family. Its aromatic leaves are often clustered near twig tips, and its small flowers develop into berry-like drupes.

Selected species: Actinodaphne mushaensis · Actinodaphne wightiana

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Cheng-Te Hsu / iNaturalist · CC BY
04

Lauraceae · Genus 02

Endiandra

Endiandra consists mainly of evergreen rainforest trees found from Southeast Asia to Australia and the Pacific. Many species have simple leaves and fleshy, single-seeded fruits.

Selected species: Endiandra sieberi · Endiandra discolor

Burseraceae · Genus 01

Canarium

Canarium includes resinous tropical trees with compound leaves and fleshy stone fruits. Several species are valued for edible fruits, fragrant resins, timber or useful oils.

Selected species: Canarium album · Canarium australasicum

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Graham Bell / iNaturalist · CC BY-NC
06

Burseraceae · Genus 02

Dacryodes

Dacryodes is a pantropical genus of evergreen trees. These plants are recognised for aromatic resin, compound foliage and fleshy fruits eaten by wildlife and people.

Selected species: Dacryodes excelsa · Dacryodes rostrata

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Armando Feliciano / iNaturalist · CC BY-NC

Myristicaceae · Genus 01

Horsfieldia

Horsfieldia contains tropical Asian evergreen trees related to nutmeg. Their fleshy fruits split open at maturity and expose a seed that is often partly surrounded by a vivid aril.

Selected species: Horsfieldia irya · Horsfieldia superba

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Ian Lockwood / iNaturalist · CC BY-NC
08

Myristicaceae · Genus 02

Knema

Knema is a genus of evergreen trees distributed across tropical Asia. Many species have reddish sap and fruits that open to reveal a dark seed wrapped in a brightly coloured aril.

Selected species: Knema attenuata · Knema conferta
