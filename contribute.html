How to Contribute

  1. Choose a plant you would like to share.
  2. Take a clear photograph of the plant.
  3. Enter the plant name and location if known.
  4. Add a short comment about your observation.
  5. Submit your contribution to Plantify.
Contribute a Plant

Plant Contribution Tips

Keep these tips in mind when making your contribution.

  • Take a clear photo of the whole plant.
  • Include a close-up of the leaves or flowers.
  • Record the location where you found the plant.
  • Do not damage or remove protected plants.
  • Write your observation while you remember it.
