Contribute to Plantify

Become a Plant Contributor

Help Plantify grow by sharing useful plant information, photos and care knowledge with other plant lovers.

How to Contribute

  1. Choose a plant you would like to contribute.
  2. Prepare useful information about the plant.
  3. Fill in the contribution form.
  4. Submit your contribution to Plantify.

What Can You Contribute?

Plant Information
Share the plant's common name, scientific name, family, genus and species.
Plant Care Tips
Provide information about watering, sunlight, soil and general plant care.
Plant Photos
Submit clear photographs that can help users identify and learn about plants.

Why Contribute?

Your contribution helps Plantify provide more useful and reliable plant information to the community.
