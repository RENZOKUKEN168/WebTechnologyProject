01

Dipterocarp family

Dipterocarpaceae

Many of Southeast Asia's tallest rainforest trees belong to this family. Dipterocarps are recognised for resinous wood and fruits with wing-like sepals.

Selected genera: Vatica · Dipterocarpus

02

Laurel family

Lauraceae

Lauraceae consists mainly of aromatic evergreen trees and shrubs. Fragrant oils are often present in their leaves, bark and wood.

Selected genera: Actinodaphne · Endiandra

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Ken-ichi Ueda / iNaturalist · CC BY
03

Torchwood family

Burseraceae

These tropical trees and shrubs frequently have aromatic bark and resin-producing tissues. The family includes plants associated with frankincense and myrrh.

Selected genera: Canarium · Dacryodes

Explore on iNaturalist ↗ Photo: Kuklín / iNaturalist · CC BY-NC
04

Nutmeg family

Myristicaceae

Myristicaceae is composed mainly of tropical evergreen trees. Its members commonly have aromatic sap, fleshy fruits and seeds.

Selected genera: Horsfieldia · Knema
