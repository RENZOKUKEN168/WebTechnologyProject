FIELD NOTES

Tips for a useful photograph

  1. Choose a well-lit image with the plant in focus.
  2. Include a close-up of leaves or flowers if possible.
  3. Record where the plant was growing.
  4. Avoid removing rare or protected plants from their habitat.

Different species can look alike. Treat visual comparisons as a starting point, not a confirmed scientific identification.

Genus

Species
A particular kind of plant within a genus.
