FIELD GUIDE

A better photo.
A better start.

  1. Find good light. Keep the plant clearly in focus.
  2. Check its features. Photograph leaves, flowers or fruit.
  3. Note the habitat. Record where it is growing.
  4. Leave it growing. Avoid removing protected plants.

Similar-looking plants may need expert verification before their identity is confirmed.

A quick identification glossary

Genus
A group of closely related species.
Species
A particular kind of plant within a genus.
